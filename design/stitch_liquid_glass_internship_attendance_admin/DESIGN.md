## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-futuristic, hyper-tactile "iOS 27 Liquid Glass" sensory paradigm tailored for modern student internship and practical field work (PKL / Magang) monitoring. Designed to elevate an everyday administrative routine into a state-of-the-art spatial ritual, the aesthetic fuses deep-space obsidian substrates with volumetric chromatic refractions, dynamic fluid glass, and bio-cybernetic telemetric data visualization.

### Aesthetic Attributes
- **Liquid Glassmorphism**: Multilayered frosted glass structures with deep backdrop blurs (32px–48px), micro-specular rim lighting, and fluid inner reflections. Surfaces emulate cut optical sapphire suspended in zero gravity.
- **Chromatic Depth & Luminance**: Ambient luminous chromatic orbs in electric cyan, ultraviolet, and emerald diffuse through obsidian darkness, providing environmental backlighting without compromising data contrast.
- **Precision Telemetry**: High-density geometric typography paired with monospaced readout clusters, evoking mission control operations, satellite geofencing locks, and biometric verification interfaces.

## Colors

The palette operates in a default hyper-dark realm. Surfaces are derived from pure spectral light passing through dark translucent media rather than opaque fills.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Electric Cyan - `#06b6d4` & `#22d3ee`)**: Denotes primary dynamic actions, active radar sweeping states, geofence boundary rings, and active spatial focus.
- **Secondary (Neon Violet - `#8b5cf6` & `#a855f7`)**: Utilized for structural accents, secondary interactive chips, dynamic island aura pulses, and secondary KPI telemetry.
- **Tertiary / Success (Bio-Emerald - `#10b981` & `#34d399`)**: Dedicated to verified biometric matches, on-time attendance validation, synchronized satellite telemetry, and authorized perimeter states.
- **Alert / Warning Systems**: Solar Amber (`#f59e0b`) handles perimeter threshold cautions and pending sign-offs. Rose Laser (`#f43f5e`) signals out-of-bounds geofence violations, biometric verification errors, or urgent compliance alerts.
- **Neutral Substrates (`#030712` to `#0a0f1d`)**: Deep space obsidian baselines layered with frosted glass fills ranging from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` to `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.70)`. Text hierarchy prioritizes pure white (`#ffffff`) for dominant titles, transitioning to silver-slate (`#94a3b8`) and attenuated muted zinc (`#64748b`).

## Typography

The typography bridges technical data readouts and editorial polish:
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Geometric, sharp, and confident. Used for operational statuses, dashboard metrics, greetings, and primary section labels.
- **Body (`Inter`)**: Neutral, ultra-legible grotesque optimized for contextual microcopy, activity audit feeds, supervisor comments, and form interaction.
- **Telemetry & Micro-Labels (`JetBrains Mono`)**: High-contrast, tabular data readouts used for WITA (UTC+8) real-time chronometers, latitude/longitude coordinate strings, hash signatures, and satellite signal telemetry badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface is driven by a flexible fluid column system overlaid on a strict 4px/8px incremental spatial scale. 

- **Layout Structure**: 4 columns on mobile (375px–639px), 8 columns on tablet (640px–1023px), and 12 columns on desktop (1024px+). The maximum layout wrapper is capped at 1280px with centered fluid auto margins.
- **Screen Real Estate Adaptation**: On mobile viewports, high-priority operational targets (Biometric Trigger & Floating Island) anchor to the screen perimeters, with cards filling full-width gutter margins. On desktop viewports, metrics expand into modular dashboard grids while the attendance telemetry stream forms a dedicated, fixed-elevation lateral panel.
- **Dynamic Breathing Room**: Margins expand gracefully across devices to allow background refractive mesh gradients to bleed softly into negative space, maintaining an expansive zero-gravity feel.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a multi-layered physical optics model rather than traditional opaque shadows. Depth is communicated via variable glass density, specular inner highlights, and ambient chromatic glow.

### 1. Base Layer (Canvas)
The root canvas is deep obsidian (`#030712`) enriched with diffuse, dynamic blur orbs (`filter: blur(80px)`) positioned behind primary interaction zones.

### 2. Glass Substrates (Cards & Containers)
- **Background**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` to `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` with backdrop filters calibrated between `blur(32px)` and `blur(48px)` and `saturate(180%)`.
- **Inner Specular Stroke**: `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.20)`, capturing simulated overhead light at the top rim of every pane.
- **Prismatic Edge**: 1px perimeter border created using semi-transparent gradients transitioning from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)` at the top-left to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` at the bottom-right.

### 3. Floating Interactive Tier (Islands, Triggers, Navbars)
- Elevated elements hover with layered ambient projection: a soft structural shadow (`0 20px 50px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`) paired with a luminous tinted chromatic aura (`0 0 30px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)` or `0 0 30px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)`).
- When active, biometric triggers and beacon states ramp up illumination with animated pulsing glow layers (`0 0 40px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The geometric identity relies on continuous superellipses and capsule geometries (Pill-shaped, roundedness tier 3), replicating organic liquid droplets and precision-molded glass hardware.

- **Dynamic Island & Micro-Pills**: 9999px border radius for real-time chronometers, telemetry pills, category filter tags, and operational states.
- **Metric Panels & Glass Containers**: Continuous curvature matching `28px` to `36px` (`rounded-xl`), creating soft, ergonomic framing that avoids harsh terminal corners.
- **Form Inputs & Action Triggers**: Fully rounded `rounded-lg` (32px) or continuous capsule geometry to enforce a friendly, tactile physical surface.

## Components

### Floating Island Header
A suspended, shape-shifting capsule anchored at the top viewport.
- **Components**: Live WITA (UTC+8) digital chronometer in `JetBrains Mono`, real-time GPS Geofence sync indicator (with pulsating emerald dot), and satellite lock status.
- **State Changes**: Expands fluidly with spring physics when geofence threshold warnings occur or during active attendance verification.

### Biometric Scan & Radar Attendance Trigger
The centerpiece of the check-in experience.
- **Visuals**: A circular frosted glass disc (`w-48 h-48`) containing concentric animated radar rings pulsing from the core. Centered with an iridescent fingerprint/retina vector glyph.
- **States**: 
  - *Idle / In Zone*: Subtle cyan refraction and slow orbital particles.
  - *Scanning*: Accelerated violet-cyan pulse rings with an active laser scan line (`#22d3ee`).
  - *Verified Success*: Snaps into bio-emerald bloom (`#10b981`), triggering dynamic haptic feedback and confirmation particle dispersion.
  - *Out of Zone*: Muted slate glass with Rose Laser boundary caution border.

### Liquid Glass Metric Cards (KPI Dashboard)
Cards display live telemetry: *Waktu Masuk*, *Kehadiran %*, *Radius Kantor (e.g. 14m / 50m)*, and *Sisa Jam Kerja PKL*.
- **Styling**: `backdrop-blur-xl`, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill, `1px` gradient specular border.
- **Visual Features**: Micro-progress arcs rendered in neon gradients, monospaced coordinate badges, and muted glow beneath current status highlights.

### Glassmorphic Floating Pill Navigation
- Suspended capsule navigation anchored at the bottom edge.
- Frosted glass housing holding glowing icon states. Active tab items feature an illuminated gradient pill backplate with micro-specular rim lighting.

### Inputs & Verification Logs
- **Text & Camera Attachments**: Glass input fields with inset drop-shadows and bright white placeholder text.
- **History List Items**: Horizontal glass strips featuring split columns: left timestamp telemetry in mono, center student/mentor activity, right biometric status indicator.
